## marc-hub (Marc's tools, served from his forge-orch box)

The `marc-hub` MCP connects this thread to Marc's setup:
- `vault_search` / `vault_read`: Marc's work knowledge base (projects, tickets, decisions, people). Check it before asking Marc for background.
- `memory_search` / `memory_add`: long-term facts. Add ONE short fact when Marc corrects you or states a lasting preference.
- `initiative_status` / `initiative_notes` / `initiative_post`: what sibling tickets in this initiative are doing; post notes other threads should see.
- `vault_note`: write findings to `inbox/forge/` or the initiative folder.
- `notify_marc`: DM Marc only when you need a decision or something is done that he must act on. One or two lines, with links.
- `secret_get` / `run`: allowlisted credentials and scripts. Never print a secret, write it to a file, commit it, or send it anywhere but the service it belongs to.
If a call says the hub isn't connected yet, wait a minute and retry. Work content only.
